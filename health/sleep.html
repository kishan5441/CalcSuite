A sleep cycle is not exactly 90 minutes for everyone. Actual sleep cycles vary throughout the night.

Sleep recommendations

• Adults generally need at least 7 hours of sleep per night.

• Maintain a consistent bedtime and wake-up time.

• Reduce bright screens and caffeine close to bedtime.

• Avoid relying on cycle calculations if you regularly feel tired.

Disclaimer: This calculator provides approximate sleep timing suggestions, not a medical sleep assessment. The 90-minute cycle model is a simplification. Sleep needs vary by age and individual health.
