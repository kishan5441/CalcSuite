Coordinate Formulas

Cartesian to Polar

r = √(x² + y²)

θ = atan2(y, x)

Polar to Cartesian

x = r cos(θ)

y = r sin(θ)

Distance

d = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]

Midpoint

M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)
